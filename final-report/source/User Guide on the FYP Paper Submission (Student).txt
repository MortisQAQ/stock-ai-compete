User Guide on the Final Year Project Paper
Submission (For regular FYP)
 1. Students can login and see the project folder from the link below.
    https://mycuhk.sharepoint.com/sites/Faculty.ERG/FYP/2026-27/CSE/Shared%20Documents/

     *You can only see your own project folder, e.g. ABC2302




 2. The format of the link is as follows.
    https://mycuhk.sharepoint.com/sites/Faculty.ERG/FYP/2026-27/CSE/Shared%20Documents/
    [Project Code]

 3. Click on the link and it will redirect to CUHK Office 365 LOGIN page.
   4. Before the deadline, the student is required to upload the reports to corresponding
      “Documents” folder. e.g. ABC2302 > Term1 > Planning

[Important Note]

       SharePoint is designated exclusively for uploading your reports.

       DO NOT upload unrelated files to SharePoint, such as datasets or AI models.

       Unrelated files will be deleted to maintain system efficiency and accessibility.




   5. The name convention of the reports is as follows.
          a. [Project Code]_[SID]_planning_report.pdf
          b. [Project Code]_ [SID]_progress_report.pdf
          c. [Project Code]_ [SID]_final_report.pdf
