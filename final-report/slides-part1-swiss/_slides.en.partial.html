ACT 01 · THE REAL QUESTION
04 / 05 · THE GAP
BEFORE REAL MONEY MOVES

Two questions remain.

WHAT ALREADY WORKS
Single-shot research
is already good.
  • Quotes, filings and earnings calls summarised in seconds.
  • Long reports read end to end, with a fluent conclusion.
  • A well-written answer — for one question, at one moment.
WHAT WE NEED TO VERIFY
Two questions
before real money.
Q1

Run it on A-share rules for three months — is the P&L and max drawdown visible?

T+1 settlement, price limits, lot sizes, fees. A continuous curve, not a single call.

Q2

Change one risk prompt or one tool — can the difference be compared honestly?

Same history, same rules: did it get steadier, or just more aggressive?

→ The question is not “is it smart?” but “is the result visible?”
